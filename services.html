Security Services

Field-Focused Protection

Security services configured around the risks, routines and responsibilities of your site.

Every environment has a different security profile. Our service design considers people, property, access, operating hours, assets, public interaction and escalation requirements before duties are defined.

Corporate Security

Professional front-of-house and building security for offices, managed properties and corporate estates.

  • Reception and entrance security
  • Visitor and contractor management
  • Staff access procedures
  • Key and pass control
  • Internal and external patrols
  • Incident response and reporting
  • Opening and closing support

Gatehouse & Industrial Security

Structured control of people and vehicles entering factories, warehouses, yards and industrial estates.

  • Vehicle and driver logging
  • Delivery and contractor checks
  • Access authorisation
  • Gate and barrier procedures
  • Key control
  • Perimeter and yard patrols
  • Out-of-hours escalation

Retail Security

Visible, customer-aware officers who support safer retail environments without compromising the customer experience.

  • High-visibility deterrence
  • Loss-prevention support
  • Entrance and exit presence
  • Incident management
  • Opening and closing procedures
  • Staff reassurance
  • Management liaison and reporting

Construction Security

Protection for active projects where access, equipment, materials and changing site conditions require consistent oversight.

  • Site entrance control
  • Contractor and visitor records
  • Boundary and perimeter checks
  • Plant and asset awareness
  • Out-of-hours guarding
  • Scheduled patrols
  • Incident escalation
